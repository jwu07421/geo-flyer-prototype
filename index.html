How this prototype is structured

  • Visual layer: the original flyer image remains visible.
  • Semantic layer: the page adds readable summaries, facts, audience terms and questions users may ask.
  • Structured layer: JSON-LD describes the merchant, offer, location, validity and project-specific fields.
  • Evidence layer: source links, update dates and limitations are shown instead of hidden claims.
