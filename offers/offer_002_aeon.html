AEON x 日清の出前一丁放題

This page tests how a retail event promotion can be represented with both visible offer text and embedded structured data.

Retail eventQuestion-answer formatJSON-LD embedded

What is this offer?

AEON and Nissin run a limited-time in-store event. Customers can purchase a selected eco bag for HK$100 and fill it with Demae Iccho products during the event window.

JSON-LD Layer

The page head describes the merchant as a store and the promotion as an offer with a minimum purchase condition, event time, and redemption method.

Schema Gap

The event rule is understandable in text, but Schema.org does not directly express a bag-filling quantity mechanic, so a custom redemption method remains useful.

Semantic persona layer

Who might be looking for this offer?

This page is relevant to supermarket and household shoppers looking for a limited-time value promotion. The visible wording connects the HK$100 purchase condition, the participating AEON branches, the event time and the Demae Iccho product context.

Questions this page answers

What do I receive?

Buy a selected eco bag for HK$100 and fill it with Demae Iccho products during the event period.

Where can I redeem it?

The promotion is available at participating AEON supermarkets. The participating-store list should be checked before visiting.

Who is this offer suitable for?

It is suitable for household shoppers, families and customers looking for a limited-time supermarket promotion.
